# Project archetypes

Choose one primary archetype and optional overlays.

## Support and maintenance

Prioritize intake reconciliation, incident/support age, SLA, repeat defects, regressions, technical coverage, PR review capacity, client response, and out-of-scope work.

## Product launch

Prioritize scope freeze, milestone and beta/release readiness, external dependencies, quality/stabilization, deployment flow, DORA measures, and decision latency.

## Discovery and validation

Prioritize hypotheses, feedback cycles, decision evidence, prototype approval, client inputs, content/assets, stakeholder availability, and confirmation gates.

## Fixed-scope delivery

Prioritize scope baseline, acceptance criteria, change control, milestone acceptance, estimate at completion, and contractual dependencies.

## Retainer and capacity

Prioritize planned allocation, actual hours, burn, remaining capacity, throughput, team changes, and forecast coverage. Roster membership is not allocation.

## Internal initiative

Prioritize outcomes, benefits, dependencies, capacity, governance, adoption, and stakeholder alignment.

## Known starting recommendations

- Sloski: `support-maintenance` with `fixed-scope-delivery` overlay.
- EverAway: `product-launch` with `retainer-capacity` overlay.
- Orodjarna: `discovery-validation` with `fixed-scope-delivery` overlay.
