# Project Operations finance

Read [source authority](../../references/source-authority.md), [connector adapters](../../references/connector-adapters.md), [PM records](../../references/pm-records.md), and [health and cadence](../../references/health-and-cadence.md).

1. Confirm the executed commercial baseline and approved estimate revision.
2. Read nominated Drive tabs/ranges and Povio Dashboard actuals using stable IDs and a bounded period.
3. Read remaining tracker work and milestone dependencies; do not estimate from PR counts alone.
4. Separate approved scope, in-scope defects, clarification, material enhancement, additional work, and uncertainty.
5. Calculate actual, committed, estimate to complete, estimate at completion, variance, burn, and unpriced change only from traceable inputs.
6. Reconcile roster, explicit allocation, absences, and actual time.
7. Preserve every contradiction and request human judgment for material commercial interpretation.
8. Store a dated budget snapshot and surface decisions or change requests.

Use Gray when the baseline is absent or stale. Do not write invoices, finance systems, estimates, or baselines without named approval.
